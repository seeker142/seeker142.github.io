<h2>Side Story 4: The Isolated Island 14</h2>

We entrusted the captured Silard spy to Genba, then continued deeper into the dungeon with Ouri-kun.

There were no visible issues on Floor 43, 44, and 45, but when we reached the end of Floor 46, where the magic tool was activated...

「What is this door...?」

「What about it?」

Ouri-kun was confused, staring at the closed door in front of us. Fran didn't seem to realize it, but it was certainly unusual.

Just like we heard beforehand, this dungeon had no bosses other than the final boss at the very bottom of the dungeon. We didn't fight any midbosses on the way here. There wasn't even anything resembling a boss room.

Yet this door was unmistakably the entrance to a boss room. It was only natural for Ouri-kun to be surprised.

『This must be the work of Silard's magic tool. To think it could create a boos room out of nowhere...』

「R-Right. It seems the structure of the dungeon has been altered.」

『We might have to prepare for the worst possible outcome.』

I had expected changes like the addition of traps, or turning the corridors into a maze. Since the top rankers weren't accustomed to dungeon exploration, that would explain why they were so late to return.

This raised a few questions though.

First, the floor. We were currently at the very end of Floor 46, while the top rankers were supposed to be farming on Floor 46. Since we haven't found them, they must have continued to Floor 47, but why?

Also, the top rankers should be strong enough to defeat the boss of this floor. But the fact that the boss was still here meant they never cleared it.

The worst possible outcome would be the top rankers challenging the boss and getting completely wiped out. Perhaps the boss turned out to be far stronger than normal.

『Well, not much point in further speculation. Let's fight it and find out.』

「Nn!」

「Woof woof!」

「I-I'll do my best not to hold you back.」

So Ouri-kun wants to come with us...

『Ouri-kun, I'll be honest with you. I have no idea how strong the boss is. We might not be able to guarantee your safety.』

「I understand. If I become a burden, feel free to leave me behind!」

「Nn.」

Ouri-kun seemed completely serious, and Fran nodded in approval of his resolve. I think Ouri-kun wouldn't complain if we did end up abandoning him.

He was just that stubborn. Well, even if Fran acted that way, she'll probably still save him if push comes to shove.

We could have told him to wait in front of the door, but Fran and Ouri-kun both seemed eager to charge inside.

『Urushi, keep an eye on Ouri-kun for me.』

（Woof.）

The boss room was shaped like an enormous dome, nearly 300 meters in diameter with an incredibly high ceiling too.

A single gigantic golem was sitting at the center. It was nearly 30 meters tall, with bulky arms and legs.

It certainly radiated an imposing presence, but I couldn't quite identify what it was made of. The metallic surface had a color similar to platinum, but it shimmered like a rainbow depending on the angle of the light.

I tried using Appraisal and was shocked by the results.

『Whoa! Demi-Orichalcos Golem?! Are you serious?!』

「Ooh!」

「Orichalcos? What's that?」

Apparently, Ouri-kun wasn't familiar with it.

『Orichalcos is a legendary metal that only divine blacksmiths can work with. Apparently, it's what all the divine swords are made out of.』

「Incredible! Such a metal exists?!」

『Based on the "demi" in the name, this guy doesn't seem to be made out of genuine orichalcos though.』

Perhaps it was just an imitation of the legendary metal. But if the entirety of that massive body was made out of the same material, just how tough is it going to be...?

「Ordinary attacks might not work against it.」

「I'll cut it and find out!」

Faced with a golem bearing the name of a legendary metal, Fran dashed forward with a sparkle in her eyes. The golem had barely started moving, so Fran launched the first attack.

「Haaaaah!」

She rushed under it and slammed me into its enormous leg.

Along with a high-pitched scraping sound, we left a big cut in the giant golem's leg. However, Fran looked dissatisfied.

（It's hard.）

『Yeah. It has an anti-physical barrier and even regeneration.』

Even if that was just a probing strike, Fran had intended to cleave the entire leg off. The fact that she failed to even cut halfway through was frustrating.

「*GOROOOOOOH!*」

「Hmph!」

The golem took a large step forward and brought its fist down toward Fran. The surprisingly smooth motion was due to its Fist Mastery skill.

『It's faster than I expected!』

Well, only in comparison to its enormous size. Fran and Urushi would never let themselves get hit by an attack of this level. The only problem might be Ouri-kun...

「Deyaaaah!」

Of course he immediately tries to attack it head-on! He's a bonafide martial artist after all!

Ouri-kun employed the Ouka Style Art: Wind Blossom. This was powerful enough to defeat an Orichalcum Golem in a single blow, but...

「Wha...?!」

His slash only managed to cut slightly into the giant golem's leg before stopping. The golem was clearly harder than orichalcum even before the barrier.

This was not like any foe he's ever faced before. Ouri-kun pulled his sword out in frustration.

「Kuh... Damn!」

He barely managed to dodge the golem's fist. However, he wasn't fast enough to escape outside the golem's range like Fran.

『Fran. Let's go straight for its core and—』

「Oi! You guys!」

Huh? A voice? Where did that come from?

「...?」

『Fran! Over there!』

I quickly looked around and spotted the source of the voice.

『Wait, the door on the other side is open?!』

Normally, the door would close and trap you inside when you entered a dungeon boss room. It was possible to escape using things like a Teleportation Feather, but generally speaking, boss rooms were designed to prevent challengers from fleeing.

However, this room seemed to be different. The entrance closed behind us, but the exit had been open the entire time. I didn't notice at all since it was hidden behind the golem's enormous body.

A man was waving at us from the open exit, apparently calling us over. Ouri-kun noticed him too, and answered back with a bit of surprise.

「Ango-san! You're safe!」

『You know him?』

「Yes! Ango-san is the Rank 5 fighter!」

It seemed like the top rankers were safe after all. Well, I guess this room would allow them to escape before getting killed.

「Oi! Come over here!」

『Fran! Ouri-kun! Forget about the golem for now! Let's regroup with them first!』

「Nn.」

「Understood!」

While I harassed the golem with Telekinesis and Earth Magic, the two of them made their way to the exit. The golem tried to pursue us at first, but it eventually stopped moving the moment we stepped outside.

After a few seconds of pause, it slowly returned to its original position. Apparently, leaving the room was enough for it to exit combat.

「Ango-san, I'm glad you're safe.」

「Ouri, what are you doing here? And could this young lady be...?」

「I'm the Rank S adventurer, Fran. And this is Urushi.」

「Woof woof!」

「Oh! Just as I thought! Your presence is every bit as formidable as the rumors suggest! Wolf-dono, your aura is just as incredible too...!」

Fran gave a smug little grin as she raised me high into the air.

「And this is Master! A super amazing divine sword!」

『I'm the divine sword, Master. Nice to meet you.』

「Ooooooh! A divine sword! Now that you mention it, it radiates such an impressive aura! So divine!」

Oh? Really? You can tell? Well, I am a divine sword now, so it's only natural that my aura is divine too!

「I am Rank 5, Ango of the 'Makeshift Blade'! Black Lightning Princess-dono, Wolf-dono, Divine Sword-dono! I am pleased to make your acquaintance!」

As expected, Ango was one of the top rankers... But that title of 'Makeshift Blade'[^1] is such a terrible name! Now that I think about it, the swordswoman and blacksmith Ouna we met a while ago sometimes gave her swords a similarly terrible name. Maybe she learned her naming sense from the founder of the Ouka Style.

**Translation Notes**

[^1]: Tsukeyakiba (付焼刃) refers to a poorly-made katana that only has a superficial edge of tempered steel. It works for a short time before losing its edge and breaking.
