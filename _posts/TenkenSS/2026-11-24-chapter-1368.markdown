<h2>Side Story 4: The Isolated Island 16</h2>

After finishing introductions, we went on to discuss our plan going forward with the top rankers.

Well, not like there was much to worry about now that we've regrouped. We just needed to beat the boss and escape the dungeon.

Apparently, the top rankers had planned on resting a little longer so they could challenge the giant golem in peak condition. Jakukei and Sousei, their strongest fighters, were still recovering from their wounds.

Now that I think about it, the serious injuries Ango mentioned must have been referring to those two.

「Such an exaggeration, I tell you. We're perfectly fine now. Right, Sousei?」

「Yes. See this, Sayuri-san? The wounds have already closed, so there's nothing to worry about.」

「Don't be silly. After suffering such terrible wounds, you have to rest for a least an entire day.」

「Yeah! Don't push yourself!」

Jakukei and Sousei insisted that they were fine, but the healers, Sayuri and Chiyo, were having none of that. Honestly, healers and doctors were the experts here. It's best to listen to them.

That said, I had a few questions for them.

『Can you tell me about the attack that injured you so badly?』

Even if it was to shield their companions, the golem nearly killed the strongest and second strongest fighters with a single attack. That sounded incredibly dangerous.

「We simply made a careless mistake. I won't get hit by it next time.」

「Well, that one might be pretty difficult to dodge. I'll be able to handle it now though.」

I should have expected as much, but these martial artists really hated losing.

Apparently, the golem sent lasers flying all over the place while they were pinned down by the magnified gravity. The two were riddled with holes all over their bodies, so it's only natural they couldn't make a complete recovery.

「Hey, can I try fighting the boss golem a bit?」

『Eh? Fran? You mean right now?』

「Nn. Since I can try as many times as I want.」

Oh, so she wanted to do the same thing as Ango. I suppose this was a relatively safe way to challenge a boss deep within the dungeon, since we could always run if things get dicey. Sounds like pretty good conditions for training.

In fact, it might actually be the perfect punching bag.

「Oh? Sounds good to me. Not like we have anything better to do.」

「Would you mind if we watch?」

「I'm interested in seeing how you fight too!」

Naturally, none of these battle maniacs intended to stop Fran. If anything, they were so enthusiastic that they all came to spectate.

We climbed the stairs and returned to the exit of the boss room. The giant golem stood motionless in the center of the room. It had its back turned, but presumably, it would turn around to attack the moment Fran stepped inside.

But right before Fran entered the room, Ouri-kun spoke up nervously.

「U-Um! Would it be all right if I went in with you too?」

He had been nervously silent around the top rankers for a while, but apparently, he finally mustered the courage to speak.

Normally, this was the type of situation where he would be told to stay out of it to avoid holding us back, but...

「Nn? Sure.」

「Ouri! You've got guts!」

「I'll pick up your bones if you die!」

All of them welcomed Ouri-kun's decision with smiles.

I've already lost track of how many times this island of martial artists surprised me. Everyone here was favorable to those willing to challenge themselves, with the understanding that it was his responsibility if it resulted in death.

「Just make sure not to push yourself too hard. Fall back before you die, okay?」

「As long as you don't die instantly, we can heal you!」

Even the healers, Sayuri and Chiyo, showed no signs of trying to stop him.

「Alright, let's go.」

「Yeah!」

Fran entered the boss room accompanied by a slightly over-enthusiastic Ouri-kun. The Boss Golem slowly began to move.

「This time, we'll cut through it!」

『Yeah! Let's do this!』

「Woof woof!」

Fran and Urushi dashed forward, with Fran targeting the right leg and Urushi targeting the left.

She vented all the accumulated frustration from her earlier failure, as well as the mistakes against the Adamantite Golems.

Fran slammed a fire-aspected Elemental Sword into the Demi-Orichalcos Golem's right leg. This was her most powerful blow today, but Fran still seemed dissatisfied.

「Hmm.」

『Still not good enough?』

「Not good at all.」

Like before, Fran's intention was to sever the golem's leg completely. But despite putting all that force into the attack, she only managed to cut about halfway through. And as if that wasn't enough, the golem regenerated practically instantly.

「Awooon...」

『You didn't manage to do much better, Urushi?』

「Arf.」

Urushi had tried to bite a leg off, but he only managed to scrape away a little off the surface.

Honestly, I'm impressed that the golem got away with such little damage against a serious attack from Fran and Urushi. It may have "demi" in the name, but orichalcos was no joke.

Well, I guess it is the metal used to make divine swords! As expected of the legendary orichalcos! This stuff is unbeatable!

「Master, you look happy for some reason.」

『Eh? N-No, not at all!』

「*Staaare*.」

『F-Fran! Look! The golem is already finished regenerating, so you can attack it as much as you want!』

「Oh right.」

「Woof!」

And so, Fran, Urushi, and Ouri-kun began their training in earnest. Well, not like there was much to it. We basically just attacked the golem and then retreated, over and over again.

Fran kept using Elemental Sword, occasionally glancing over at Ouri-kun. She must be keeping an eye on him in order to cover for his mistakes.

Meanwhile, Ouri-kun gradually lost his excitement when he realized that his attacks were not very effective. His Mana Blade may be powerful enough to destroy an Adamantite Golem, but it probably wasn't enough against the Demi-Orichalcos Golem.

「Dammit... Is this my limit...?」

Eventually, he stopped attacking altogether. His face was twisted with despair at his own lack of strength. But then, a voice of encouragement called out to him.

「No, Ouri! Don't stop attacking.」

「Fran-dono...」

It was Fran, of all people. I was probably the most surprised! To think she would try to cheer someone up in this situation! You've grown so much, Fran!

『Keep trying! I'm sure you can do even better than that!』

「R-Right!」

Good! I'm cheering for you too!

Ouri-kun got back up and redoubled his efforts. He refused to get discouraged when his attacks didn't work, instead pouring even more mana into his next strike of Mana Blade.

As if spurred on by Ouri-kun's efforts, Fran continued attacking as well.

「*GROOOOH!!*」

「Not yet.」

「Uroogh! Take this! Daryah!」

Neither Fran nor Ouri-kun backed down even a single step against the golem.

「Hah!」

『Oh? That cut went a little deeper than before.』

「Still not good enough.」

『...I see.』

「Nn!」

Fran continued keeping an eye on Ouri-kun as she persistently targeted the giant golem's legs. Though I doubt she had any intention of hindering its mobility. It was probably just sheer stubbornness. She wanted to cut off a leg and nothing was going to stop her.

Her relentless concentrated attack on the legs used various types of Elemental Sword. And since her strikes gradually penetrated deeper and deeper, she had probably attained a new mastery over the Elemental Sword skill.

This was a skill we've used all over our adventures, but she seemed to be focusing exclusively on it in order to grasp something new. Maybe watching Ouri-kun's usage of Mana Blade contributed to this?

Meanwhile, in addition to serving as the catalyst for Fran's growth, Ouri-kun seemed to be improving on his own as well.

「*GOGOH!*」

「Seyaaah!」

Just now, he narrowly dodged the giant golem's fist and delivered a slash to its arm, albeit a shallow one. His evasive and offensive skills seemed to have improved another level.

Perhaps this was because concentrating on a powerful opponent got rid of his unnecessary thoughts.

Ouri-kun was an earnest young man who was prone to worrying, and that sometimes worked against him. Even in the middle of the combat, he kept thinking about the movements of himself and his opponent, and tried to assess whether or not his decisions had been correct.

Never thinking was bad too, of course, but Ouri-kun was the type to overthink things to the point that he doubted his own intuition. And against a high level opponent, you can't afford to stop and think about every little thing.

But that changed when he charged in headfirst at the giant golem, an enemy far stronger than him. This action probably helped to empty his mind by just the right amount.

As a result, he quickly learned how to trust his instincts. His talents were formidable to begin with, so he might be able to grow by leaps and bounds with the right catalyst.

You're doing great, Ouri-kun! Keep it up!

I always feel strangely excited when I see someone growing right before my eyes. This sentiment was probably shared, since the top rankers started jumping into the fray too. They could no longer resist the urge to fight.

「Urooogh! Let me fight too!」

「Me too!」

「I'd like to join as well!」

「No, you can't! Sousei-san! Jakukei-san! Please, stop!」

「We'll have to punish them for this later!」
