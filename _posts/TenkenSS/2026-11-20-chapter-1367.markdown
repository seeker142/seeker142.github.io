<h2>Side Story 4: The Isolated Island 15</h2>

「So you're trapped here because you can't defeat that golem?」

「Correct. That is basically our current situation.」

Ango explained the circumstances of the top rankers.

Apparently, the disturbance in the dungeon became obvious during their trip to gather adamantite. There happened to be a minor earthquake beforehand, so they were already on their guard.

Their search revealed the boss door and they charged in together. I wouldn't call that decision careless though. They were a party of the strongest fighters on the island. If they couldn't break through the boss room, then literally no one could.

The top rankers challenged the giant golem boss, but they failed to defeat it. As a result, they had no choice but to retreat (or maybe advance?) deeper into the dungeon.

Though the golem was certainly very durable, it didn't seem that strong to me...

The top rankers apparently thought the same thing at first. But once they drove the golem into a corner, a certain gimmick was activated in the boss room.

「It was like an invisible weight applied to the entire room. Our bodies became several times heavier, which slowed us down a lot. Meanwhile, that damn golem kept moving around at the same speed.」

Could it be a sort of gravity multiplier? A debuff like that would slow anyone down. In that case, I don't blame them for running. Especially since the gravity would make their weapons heavier too.

「As you can see, we managed to escape by leaving the room. That's why we have no casualties yet. But we can't exactly go challenging it again without a plan.」

In addition, one of them suffered serious injuries after shielding a companion from the golem, and they were currently waiting for their recovery.

The healers had already treated their injuries, but that wasn't enough to restore lost blood and stamina.

「Meanwhile, I had nothing to do but wait, so I came over to check on things a bit.」

Surprisingly, Ango was not just keeping an eye on the golem. He also launched a few attacks on it for practice. Every time he got tired, he promptly retreated outside the room for a break.

「So the others are resting nearby?」

「Yeah. A bit further ahead.」

We descended the stairs after the boss room to reach Floor 47, but it didn't seem much different from Floor 46. Even the enemies were the same.

「Hmm, a golem.」

「Oh? Wow! You took it down instantly!」

Fran spotted an Adamantite Golem and immediately dashed forward to cleave it in half. Ango seemed genuinely surprised. After all, she had just sliced through adamantite, the hardest metal, without any hint of difficulty.

「Hmm?」

『What's wrong, Fran?』

「Elemental Sword wasn't that good. I'll do better next time.」

Fran was using a fire-aspected Elemental Sword, but it turned out to be less effective than she expected. I do remember hearing that adamantite was resistant to both fire and water.

Either way, she wasn't satisfied even after killing the hardest golem in one hit. That's my Fran! Always striving for more!

We continued following Ango and, just like he said, we found a group of six men and women. Not like we needed a guide though. The path was just a single corridor that we walked down for about five minutes or so.

There were no traps along the way either. It seemed that the effects of Silard's magic tool were limited to the boss room. Which was probably why such a vicious boss spawned there.

The top rankers were not resting at a designated safe zone or anything like that. They were just sitting right in the middle of a long corridor. This spot was probably chosen so they could quickly spot any approaching golems.

「Hey guys! We've got reinforcements! I'm sure we can break through next time!」

「Ango, could that young lady be...?」

「Hahaha! That's right, old man Jakukei, she's the Black Lightning Princess!」

「Well met! Please forgive our lack of hospitality, but I am honored to meet you!」

The bulky old man standing in the center greeted us with a volume as high as his imposing physique. The others seemed a bit surprised, but they also bowed their heads.

Not a single one of them complained about the appearance of a young girl like Fran. They must have recognized her strength just from her movements and gear.

『Hmm...』

「Master, what's wrong?」

『Nothing much. I was just thinking about how flashy they look...』

「...?」

How should I put it? All of them except Ango had very *distinctive* appearances, including the two healers.

To be honest, Ango himself looked rather plain. His face was pretty unremarkable, and outside of his muscles, he had an average build and height. He wore an extremely practical set of leather armor over his worn-out training clothes, making him the splitting image of a martial artist, which we had seen countless examples of since coming to this island. I could have sworn I saw a few people who looked exactly like Ango before entering the dungeon.

On the other hand, the other four top rankers and the two healers were all dressed so flamboyantly that I could never forget them.

The first to introduce themselves were the tall woman on the far right and the petite woman beside her, who wore identical outfits. Their clothes looked like a kimono-style maid uniform from the Taisho era. They looked like they came right out of an old Japanese romance story. Incredibly out of place in a dungeon like this.

「It is a pleasure to meet you. My name is Sayuri and I am a healer.」

「Hiya! I'm Chiyo, also a healer!」

「Chiyo! Mind your manners!」

「Ow! S-Sorry! I'm Chiyo, nice to meet you!」

Was Sayuri her mother or something? The two shared very similar features. The mother, Sayuri, looked to be in her forties, while her daughter, Chiyo, seemed to be around twenty.

「I suppose I will go next.」

Next was the woman standing next to Chiyo, who was about the same height as her. What stood out was the cloth blindfold covering her eyes. She performed a graceful bow while introducing herself.

「I am Rank 4, Maya of the 'Moonlit Haze'. Pleased to meet you.」

「You know, Maya is insanely strong even if she can't see. I've lost to her before.」

「Ufufu. It sounds like I'm being called a tomboy. A little embarrassing.」

Maya was apparently blind, and yet she managed to hold onto Rank 4. Impressive. The naginata she carried like a walking staff must be her weapon, the Moonlit Haze.

Next was the elderly woman standing next to Maya. She seemed to be around the same age as Ouna.

「I am Rank 3, Shima of the 'Cicada Song'.」

Her white hair was tied at the back of her head, and her face was covered in deep wrinkles. At first glance, she didn't seem capable of fighting at all. Yet her back remained perfectly straight, and there wasn't the slightest trace of age in her aura.

Though she still looked incredibly out of place in a dungeon.

「My turn next! I am Rank 2! Jakukei of the 'Drunken Stagger'! I look forward to your acquaintance!」

Next was the huge old man who greeted us earlier, a bald giant covered in bulging muscles. His clothes was nothing but a thin coat and a pair of skin-tight shorts.

His weapon must be the long sword strapped to his back, which was over two meters in length. It radiated such an incredible aura even when sheathed that I lost the will to comment on the ridiculous name. Just imagining this guy swinging that long sword around made me feel sorry for the other martial artists.

Hold on, Jakukei seemed so formidable, but he's only Rank 2. Which means...

「Gahahaha! Come on! You're up next!」

「W-Wait, Jakukei-san! Please don't slap my back! Ow, that hurts!」

Jakukei slapped the back of the last man, forcing him to step forward. He looked to be in his fifties and carried two swords at his waist, which was rare on this island.

At first glance, he seemed like a dashing gentleman with neatly parted black hair and a small mustache above his lip. I think he'd look perfect dressed as a butler, but instead he wore a black kimono. Despite his height of around 170 cm, he gave a much larger impression. Such was the power of his presence. This was clearly someone with unmatched strength. Well, not that Rank 1 could possibly be weak.

「I am Rank 1, Sousei of the 'Two-Faced Tongue'. It's a pleasure to meet you.」

「Nn!」

Fran looked over the top rankers with sparkling eyes. She might suggest a mock battle at any moment.

「I'm the adventurer Fran. This is Urushi!」

「Woof woof!」

「And this super amazing sword is the divine sword Master!」

『I'm Fran's partner! She calls me Master. Pleased to meet you.』

I gave them the same sort of introduction I'd given Ango.

「Great! I'm looking forward to our match already!」

「A divine sword! This is my first time seeing one!」

「Ufufu. Such incredible mana. Simply wonderful.」

「What a sight to behold!」

Jakukei, Sousei, Maya, and Shima showered me with praise one after another. The genuine surprise from the top rankers gave Fran an especially triumphant grin.

After all, there were six people to be impressed this time. Her smile became six times as smug.

「Hehe! That's right! Master is super amazing! The strongest divine sword ever!」

However, I could see an underlying motive in their eyes. They were trying to gauge my true strength. All of them were true battle maniacs.

And it wasn't just the top rankers. I sensed the same thing from Sayuri and Chiyo too. That means their healers can fight too. I should have expected as much from an island of martial artists.
